1Rising Dough redesign · desktop + mobile

Direction: the editorial / Swiss base is kept and tightened (warm paper, one indigo accent, serif display, mono amounts), organised around one question, "what can I spend today". Navigation drops to Today · Ledger · Plan · Accounts, with Settings below. Plan merges Budget and Forecast. Quick add lives in the top bar, on n and in ⌘K. Figures are the real ones from your screenshots; the few illustrative values (some overrides, reconcile bank balance) are marked in the rationale.

System · direction, components, IA
1aVisual direction
Visual direction

Ledger paper, one ink-blue accent

An editorial, hairline-first surface. Hierarchy comes from type scale and whitespace, not boxes. Colour is reserved for meaning: indigo for action and focus, and four directional hues that always travel with a label and glyph.

Elevation & shape

Hairlines (1px, 90% L) over shadows. Cards are 10px radius, buttons 6px, pills fully round. A single shadow exists, for overlays: 0 12px 40px / 14%. Focus ring: 2px indigo with 2px paper offset.

Directions
↓ Expense↑ Income⇄ Transit± Adjust

User colour overrides tint the category pill only; the direction badge stays semantic.

Motion

120–180 ms ease-out. Used for state only: a promoted row collapses and its amount slides into "spent"; the hero number counts to its new value; overlays rise 8px. Everything is CSS or a few lines of JS, and is off under prefers-reduced-motion.

Contrast

Body ink on paper 15:1; muted text 5.2:1; pills ≥ 4.8:1. No state is colour-only.
